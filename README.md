One line roasting a smart fridge, written by `main.py` to `entry.txt`.

> My smart fridge caught me eating cake at 2 a.m., emailed my doctor, then ran out of ice. It can judge me — it just can't chill.

The entry is a single line (127 characters), well inside the 200-character limit.
The roast is aimed at the fridge itself — its camera, its snitching, its ice
maker — and the punchline turns on the fridge failing at its one real job:
it can judge, it just can't chill.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=output python3 main.py
```
